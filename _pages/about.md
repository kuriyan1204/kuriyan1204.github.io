I am a machine learning engineer at Apple and a Ph.D. student in the [TohokuNLP group](https://www.nlp.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/). I'm interested in efficient language model training, inference, and deployment and their underlying principles.

I was previously a founding ML engineer at [Kotoba Technologies](https://www.kotoba.tech), where I worked on multilingual TTS models at scale and backend systems for the [world's fastest simultaneous translation app](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/同時通訳-リアルタイム音声翻訳の新境地/id6740851285). I was also a JSPS Research Fellow (DC1).

I interned as a software engineer at [Apple](https://www.apple.com) in Cupertino (summer 2024) and Tokyo (summer 2025), working on various NLP and image generation projects. During my undergrad and master's studies, I contributed to early efforts on [training Japanese large language models](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18wMZFRphuwRwOfeDmVlnBxxVOKjt1PVw/edit) and on [deep state space models](https://speakerdeck.com/kurita/mamba).

For the latest CV, please visit [my LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hiroto-kurita/).
You can also find me on [X](https://twitter.com/hiroto_kurita), [GitHub](https://github.com/kuriyan1204), and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=72QbISAAAAAJ&hl=en).
