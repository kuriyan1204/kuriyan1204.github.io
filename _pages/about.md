I am a Ph.D. student and [JSPS research fellow (DC1)](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-pd/) at [TohokuNLP group](https://www.nlp.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/). I'm interested in efficient LM training/inference/deployment and their underlying principles.

I was previously a founding ML engineer at [Kotoba Technologies](https://www.kotoba.tech), where I worked on multilingual TTS model at scale & backend works for [simultaneous translation app](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/同時通訳-リアルタイム音声翻訳の新境地/id6740851285). Also, I was a software engineer intern at [Apple](https://www.apple.com) in Cupertino (2024 summer) and Tokyo (2025 summer), where I worked on various NLP & image generation related projects for [Apple Intelligence](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/).
